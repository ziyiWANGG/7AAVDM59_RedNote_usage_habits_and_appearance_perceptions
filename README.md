# 7AAVDM59_RedNote_usage_habits_and_appearance_perceptions

This survey aims to understand the relationship between users' usage habits, content browsing preferences, and appearance-related perceptions on RedNote. The survey is anonymous and used solely for course research purposes, with no commercial intent.


## Research Hypotheses:

H1: The frequency of users’ engagement in appearance-related activities on RedNote (e.g., photo editing, posting selfies, actively following beauty / fitness / body-shaping bloggers, and browsing relevant content) is positively correlated with their level of body image distress.

H2: Users with high anxiety levels experienced significantly higher levels of body image distress after browsing RedNote compared to users with low anxiety levels.

H3: Users with high body image distress are more likely to browse appearance-related content (weight loss/fitness) on RedNote.


## Measurement Scales:

GAD‑7: Generalized Anxiety Disorder 7-item scale (used for anxiety level classification).
Note: GAD‑7 scores are used for research grouping only and do not represent clinical diagnosis.

BSQ‑8A: Body Shape Questionnaire 8A (used to measure body image distress).
